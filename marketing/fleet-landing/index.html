How we help

Support at every stage of the sale.

Fleet buying has a lot of moving parts. Here is where our team steps in, from the first call to the next order.

1

Understand the need

We start with the job: crew size, payload, towing, terrain and your budget cycle. You get a short list of right-sized options, not a catalog.

2

Spec the right truck

We build the Ford configuration with you: model, cab, bed, powertrain and the options worth paying for. You see the Ford spec proposal and window sticker, so you know exactly what is being ordered.

3

Plan the upfit and accessories

Service bodies, racks and lighting are coordinated with trusted upfitters such as Knapheide, Reading, Lehr and Stringfellow. Factory accessories like bed covers, running boards and floor liners go on the same quote.

4

Contract pricing, in writing

Your quote reflects State of Tennessee Statewide Contract pricing with every line shown clearly. Taxes, title and registration are quoted separately.

5

Approval and ordering

We match your agency's FIN code, prepare the paperwork and place the order. Need to add equipment? A quote can be reopened and updated, and it keeps its quote number.

6

Build and upfit tracking

We follow your order through production and upfit and keep you posted on timing, so nothing about your delivery date is a surprise.

7

Delivery and handoff

Your vehicles arrive ready to work, with a walk-through for your drivers and fleet staff.

8

Service and the next order

We stay your contact for questions and service coordination, and we help plan next year's replacements.

Questions

Good questions we hear.

What is State Contract pricing, and who can use it?

The State of Tennessee Statewide Contract sets pricing for eligible agencies. [Confirm eligibility wording with your contract documents.] Tell us who you are and we will confirm your pricing.

How long does an order take from quote to delivery?

It depends on whether we have a matching unit in stock, whether it is a factory order, and whether it needs an upfit. [Add typical timeframes.] We give you a realistic date when you quote.

Can you coordinate the upfit and accessories on the same quote?

Yes. Upfit equipment from our upfitter partners and factory accessories appear as lines on the same quote, with a description of what is included.

What do you need from us to start?

The vehicle and job you have in mind, your agency name and FIN code, and your timeline. A Ford spec proposal helps if you already have one.

Can a quote be changed after we get it?

Yes. We can reopen it to add or remove upfit equipment or options. It stays under the same quote number.

Ready to talk fleet?

Tell us what you need to put on the road. We will come back with a clear, itemized quote.
